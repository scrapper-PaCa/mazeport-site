A puzzle game for Android where every maze wraps around a 3D shape.

Coming soon to Google Play

How it plays

  1. Swipe

    The ball rolls until it hits a wall. One swipe is one move.

  2. Roll over the edge

    Cross onto the next face of the shape and keep going.

  3. Reach the portal

    Beat the par for 3 stars. Use a hint or the eye view when you're stuck.

Puzzles that grow with you

  • Crystals Collect them all to wake the portal.
  • Doors and plates Stop on a plate to open its coloured doors.
  • Teleporters Jump to the matching gate, even on another face.
  • Arrow tiles They keep you rolling in their direction.
  • Crumbling tiles They break behind you, so plan ahead.
