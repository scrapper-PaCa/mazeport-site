PRIVACY POLICY

Mazeport, by scrapper. Effective 1 November 2026.

This policy explains what data the Android game Mazeport ("the game") uses, and why.

Data the game keeps

The game has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your progress and settings (levels unlocked, stars, hints, options) are saved only on your phone. They are not sent to us, and they are deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the game.

Ads

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: optional videos you choose to watch for a hint or extra looks, and an ad between some levels. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • your approximate location, worked out from your IP address,
  • information about your device and the app, and how you interact with the ads,
  • crash and performance data of the ads SDK.

Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on the phone).

Your consent

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Options > Ad privacy choices. Without consent you may still see non-personalised ads.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
